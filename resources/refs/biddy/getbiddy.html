How it works

No treasury discretion. No manual claims.

The split is readable from the token's fee config. Nothing here depends on trusting the site.

  1. 01

    Launch on pump.fun

    Pick a collection, name your coin, add an image, sign twice: create, then the fee split. The coin goes live on pump.fun immediately, as a normal pump.fun coin.

  2. 02

    You are the creator

    You are the curve's on-chain creator, exactly as on pump.fun. Only the creator can set a fee split, so you sign it yourself — no biddy key ever holds the fees.

  3. 03

    80% to the sweep wallet

    Right after the create, you sign pump.fun's update_fee_shares yourself: 80% to the collection's sweep wallet, 20% to the protocol. The program then revokes the admin, so the split cannot change.

  4. 04

    The wallet buys the floor

    The sweep wallet does one thing: buy verified NFTs from the paired collection at the floor. Every purchase is a Solscan link, credited to the coin whose fees paid for it.

  5. 05

    You choose what happens to the NFTs

    Hold them in the vault, burn them, airdrop them to holders by balance, run a one-ticket-per-wallet lottery, or hand them out yourself as giveaways. Set it at launch, change it any time with a signature. Draws use a recorded blockhash, so anyone can recompute the winner.
